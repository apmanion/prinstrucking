About Prins Trucking

Family owned and operated.

Prins is a family business, and we run it that way: personal service, dependable work and a long-term view of every customer and every driver.

Meet the owner

Adam Dickerson

Adam is an experienced business leader with a strong background in agricultural operations, logistics and customer service. As Vice President of Operations at Konos Inc., he oversaw all aspects of egg production, with a focus on operational excellence, animal welfare and safety.

Throughout his career, Adam has emphasized building strong customer relationships, delivering dependable service and finding ways to improve efficiency and drive sustainable business growth.

He brings a customer-first mindset to his leadership. Long-term success, he believes, is built on trust, consistency and a commitment to meeting and exceeding customer expectations.

What he brings to Prins

Food-chain experience

Years running operations in egg production, so he understands what the freight is and why handling matters.

A safety-first approach

Operational excellence and safety have been priorities throughout his career, and they shape how Prins runs.

Customer first

Trust, consistency and dependable service are the foundation for every customer relationship.

Education & background
