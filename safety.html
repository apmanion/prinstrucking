Safety & Maintenance

Safety is never an afterthought at Prins.

Every load is only as good as the truck, the driver and the habits behind it. That's why safety sits at the center of how we train, how we monitor and how we maintain every unit in our fleet.

Our Safety Manager

Kurt Offringa

Kurt has been part of this operation since 1994, and his 32 years of experience guide our safety program, driver coaching and compliance practices every day.

When you work with Prins, you're working with people who know the road and take responsibility for getting everyone home safely.

Technology that helps prevent accidents

We invest in tools that help us spot risk early and keep our drivers protected.

Electronic logs

Electronic logging keeps hours-of-service records accurate and compliant, so our drivers stay rested and legal.

AI forward-facing dash cams

Automated, AI-powered cameras flag risky situations as they happen. They give us real data for coaching, and a clear record of what happened if something does.

Maintained in-house, done right

We don't hand our equipment off and hope for the best. Our own maintenance team, Jeff Warren and Daniel Warners, keeps the fleet up to date, DOT compliant and road-ready. Because the people servicing our trucks work alongside the people driving them, small issues get caught and fixed before they become big ones.
